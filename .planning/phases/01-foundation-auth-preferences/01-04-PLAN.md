<decisions>
Two gray areas UI-SPEC left open are resolved here, and one consequence is surfaced rather than
silently smoothed over:

- **64 px spacing (UI-SPEC §Spacing Scale, flagged open):** the scale is extended to `space-16`
  (64 px) rather than composing `space-12 + space-4` at call sites. UI-SPEC offered both; a
  dedicated token keeps every call site reading one token.
- **12 px (`space-3`):** authored as a real step of the numeric scale rather than a literal
  exception. Setting Tailwind v4's `--spacing` base to 4 px generates the whole numeric ladder
  (`p-1` … `p-16`) mechanically, so 12 px is `p-3` with no special case and no fifth outlier
  value. This satisfies UI-SPEC's "do not generalize `space-3` elsewhere" note in effect: nothing
  is generalized, the ladder simply exists.
- **Doubled utility segments — accepted consequence, not a defect.** D-02 and D-17 lock the
  semantic token names `color-bg-primary`, `color-text-danger`, `color-border-danger`. Inside
  Tailwind v4's `--color-*` namespace those become the utilities `bg-bg-primary`,
  `text-text-danger`, `border-border-danger`. The doubled segment is the mechanical result of
  keeping the role inside the developer's locked token name. The locked names win; the utility
  spelling is recorded here so no later plan "tidies" it into a drift.
</decisions>
