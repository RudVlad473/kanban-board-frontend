<objective>
SUBTASK-02: the completion toggle, optimistic, with a per-subtask in-flight lock and a rollback that
reaches the card's caption as well as the checkbox.

Purpose: the caption is a derived value on a different surface from the control that changes it, and
a rollback that reverts the checkbox but leaves the card reading a stale count is the defect the
UI-SPEC names as most likely here. And a version-carrying write with no in-flight lock will conflict
against itself on a double-click.
Output: a toggle that flips instantly, locks per subtask, and rolls back both surfaces together.
</objective>
