<objective>
TASK-03: editing a task's title and description, reached from the detail view's kebab, committed by
one submit on one entity — and deliberately NOT as the mock's single batched save across the task and
its versioned subtask children.

Purpose: the update DTO declares no title bounds at all, so a title that could never have been
created would otherwise become savable. And the modal closes on submit, which means the card is the
only surface a rollback can be seen on.
Output: an edit modal with the autosave hint that makes its split legible, and an optimistic save
that rolls back onto the card.
</objective>
