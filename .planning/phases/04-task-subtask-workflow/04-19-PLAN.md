<objective>
SUBTASK-01, SUBTASK-03 and SUBTASK-04: add, inline rename, and delete a subtask on an existing task,
each its own immediate mutation with its own rollback.

Purpose: this is where the per-item save rule becomes visible. The edit modal's rows look exactly
like the create modal's draft rows and behave completely differently, and the delete has no confirm
because a subtask destroys nothing beneath it — both are deliberate and both are easy to get wrong by
analogy.
Output: a live editable row, three hooks, and one new action.
</objective>

    - Each hook's override and in-flight lock are keyed on the subtask id, so operations on different rows are independent.
    - A stale-version rejection on rename or delete reverts, raises the version-conflict toast, and the board is re-read by the action.

    Key every override and every in-flight lock on the subtask id, so operations on different rows do
