<objective>
Convert the five RTL-cleanup UI test files (17 of the phase's 79 composed-story runner call sites) to
direct composed-story rendering and retire their `afterEach(cleanup)` hooks.

This batch is separated from plan 02.2-02 because its cleanup story is genuinely different: these
five delegate to `@testing-library/react`'s `cleanup()`, which only unmounts trees RTL's own
`render` created. Once no composed story renders through RTL, that hook becomes dead code — and the
four overlay/portal primitives here (dropdown, menu, modal, toast) are the ones most likely to
expose a real leak if the centralized body wipe is not sufficient.

Purpose: close 17 call sites and prove the centralized cleanup holds for portal-rendering
primitives, which is the riskiest part of D-04.
Output: five converted test files, all passing at both viewports with no per-file cleanup.
</objective>
