<objective>
A board switch currently lands the new board pre-scrolled: the horizontal column row is one DOM
element reused across switches, so board A's `scrollLeft` carries into board B whenever B also
overflows. Under `scroll-smooth` that is the most plausible source of the reported millisecond
horizontal-scroll flicker.

Purpose: a switch should open the next board at the start of its column row, with nothing to
animate.
Output: a one-element fix at the `BoardScreen` -> `BoardView` boundary, pinned by an e2e case that
is falsified against the unfixed code before the fix lands.
</objective>

<measured_facts>
Everything below was measured against the running app or read out of the repo at planning time.
Do not re-derive it; do not contradict it without measuring first.

1. `BoardScreen` is mounted by the dashboard layout, which Next does NOT re-render when only the
   `[boardId]` segment changes (its own header comment records this). So `BoardView` is reused
   across a switch, and the scroll row inside it is ONE DOM element — element identity confirmed
   constant across three switches.
2. Reproduced: scroll board A's row to `scrollLeft=400`, click board B in the sidebar where B also
   overflows -> B renders at `scrollLeft=400` and stays there. When B does NOT overflow, the
   browser clamps to 0 in the same frame — which is why BOTH seeded boards must overflow or the
   test passes for the wrong reason.
3. The sub-frame flicker itself was NOT reproduced in headless Chromium (no intermediate painted
   frame at rAF granularity, and headless hides scrollbars). The carried-over offset is the one
   confirmed scroll-state defect on a switch. Do not claim the flicker is fixed; claim the offset
   is.
4. The scroll row is `div.flex.min-h-0.flex-1.gap-6.overflow-x-auto.scroll-smooth` in
   `board-view.tsx` (~line 225), inside the `columnCount > 0` branch of the `DndContext`.
5. Geometry: a column is `w-70` (280px), the row is `gap-6` (24px) and `p-6` (24px), and the
   `AddColumnPlaceholder` ghost column is another `w-70` inside the row. Five real columns is
   ~1848px of content — overflowing 1280px (the `e2e` project's Desktop Chrome viewport) with or
   without the sidebar, and leaving well over 400px of scroll range.
6. All of `BoardView`'s own state is board-scoped: `openTaskId`, `columnBeingRenamed`,
   `columnBeingDeleted`, `taskBeingDeletedId`, `retryColumnName`, the drag session and the new
   column reveal. None of it is meaningful on a different board. `useUnconfirmedIds` re-derives
   from the mutation cache, and `useQuery`'s `initialData` re-seeds from the board just read, so a
   remount costs no fetch and shows no skeleton.
7. `board-screen.tsx` already carries the coverage pointer `Covered by:
   e2e/boards-switch.e2e.spec.ts`, so extending that spec keeps `pnpm coverage:check` satisfied.
   `board-view.tsx` has a co-located `board-view.test.tsx` and needs no pointer.
8. `security_enforcement` is `false` in `.planning/config.json` — no threat model section is
   required, and this change crosses no trust boundary.
</measured_facts>
