Why 3 and 4 are different in kind, not just in degree. Options 1 and 2 decorate a gap. Options 3 and 4 are the gap: the board is an RSC fetch behind a Suspense boundary, so there is a real wait whose length you do not control. Animating over it means the animation and the wait fight each other — on a fast connection the flourish is gratuitous, on a slow one it finishes and leaves you staring at nothing. Making the skeleton and the stagger the transition means it reads as designed at every latency, and it costs no experimental dependency: it is a Suspense boundary per column plus a CSS enter animation.

Which one?

A

4 — directional + streaming

Spatial meaning and honest about the fetch. My recommendation.

B

3 — streaming only

Skip the directional axis; just make the load feel designed.

C

2 — column wipe

The most assertive. Accept the risk that it drags with many columns.

D

1 — directional only

Simple, spatial, cheapest of the four.
