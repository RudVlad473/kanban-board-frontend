What today does
  • Elevation carried entirely by one diffuse shadow that is nearly invisible on #F4F7FD and completely invisible in dark mode
  • 23px vertical padding — only 3 cards fit before scrolling
  • Hover changes the title colour and nothing else; the card itself is inert
  • Subtask progress is prose you have to read
What changes
  • Hairline border replaces the shadow as the primary edge — works identically in both themes. Shadow demoted to a 1px ambient hint
  • Density: 14px padding, 10px gutter — ~40% more cards visible
  • Hover is a real affordance: border brightens, 1px lift, purple grab-rail appears on the left edge. 130ms
  • Progress becomes glanceable — a 3px bar plus tabular 2/3
  • Purple is now spent on only focus, grab-affordance and progress
