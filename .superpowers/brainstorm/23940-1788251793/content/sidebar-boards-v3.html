The outline, collapsed by default — and three ways to survive 12 columns

Every board starts collapsed. The chevron is a separate control from the row, so clicking the name still navigates. Roadmap carries 12 columns and is the one to open in all three.

1 · Cap at 6, then “+N more”

Shows six, then one overflow row that reveals the rest in place. Six is not arbitrary — it is the column palette's own ceiling, past which the dots repeat and stop telling columns apart. Two states, never an endless one.
