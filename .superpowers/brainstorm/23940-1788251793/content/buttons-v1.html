Buttons — the three options, plus what we ship today

Actually press them. The whole difference lives in the press, which lasts 90ms — tick slow motion to stretch every transition 6× so you can see what your hand is feeling.

Today

Pill, flat fill, colour-only hover. No press state exists at all — clicking changes nothing.

6 cva rows, one animated property.

Option 1 — material + press

4px radius, inset top highlight, hairline edge, 1px lift shadow. Press sinks the fill and inverts the highlight into an inner shadow. No geometry moves.

No rule change. Recommended.

Option 2 — plus 1px depress

Everything in option 1, plus a solid 2px bottom edge the button drops onto when pressed. The most tactile, and the most opinionated.

Needs rule 1 amended to allow press geometry.

Option 3 — accent motion

A sheen sweeps across on click. Included so you can see what I was rejecting, not as a real candidate.

Decorative, no causal grounding — same reasoning that killed the parallax.
