Today: a 369×160 card holding an h1, one sentence, and two bare anchors with background: rgba(0,0,0,0) and border-width: 0px — 4.5% of a 1440×900 viewport, and no brand mark anywhere in the DOM. The headline copy above is a proposal, not a decision: the Phase 1 UI-SPEC's Copywriting Contract covers auth form strings but says nothing about landing copy, so this is another gap rather than a departure.

Below the md breakpoint the split collapses: the board panel is dropped entirely rather than stacked under the pitch, since a cropped board at 375px wide reads as a rendering bug. Mobile gets the brand lockup, headline, sub-copy and the CTAs.
