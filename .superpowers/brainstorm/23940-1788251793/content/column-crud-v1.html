Create, rename, delete — one surface

Reorder — does the card lift survive at column scale?

§2 adopted scale(1.03) for a dragged card. A card is ~250×74; a column is 240×300+, so the same ratio moves its far edge much further. These are the three candidates at real column size, held in the lifted state.
