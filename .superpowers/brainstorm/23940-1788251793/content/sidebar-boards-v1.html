The sidebar board list, and creating a board into it

The list is the containing surface and the create flow is what adds to it, so they are one question: what does a row look like, and how does a new one arrive. Hover and click the rows in every column — they are all live.

The row

A · Today

44px rows, 8px apart, a full-bleed purple slab for the open board. Every row carries exactly one thing: its name.

    B · Rail + count

    36px rows, 4px radius, selection is a 2px purple rail plus a tint rather than a slab. A tabular task count earns the space the slab gave back.

    All boards (3)

      C · Rail + progress

      B, plus a 2px completion bar per board — the same 3px card bar at sidebar scale. The list stops being an index and starts being a status view.

      All boards (3)

        D · Rail + column signature

        B, plus one dot per column in that board's own column colours — a fingerprint, so boards are told apart by shape as well as by reading.
