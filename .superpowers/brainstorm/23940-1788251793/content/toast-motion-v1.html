Toast enter and exit — G3

Everything toast.tsx ships today is a cut: one transition-colors on the close button and nothing else. Base UI publishes a whole stacking system (--toast-index, --toast-offset-y, data-expanded) that src references nowhere.

Click a toast's body to dismiss it, or leave it for the 5s timeout — the two exits are deliberately different. Dismiss the middle one of three to see the others close the gap. Raise eight and close them one at a time: the queue surfaces, three at a time, newest first.
