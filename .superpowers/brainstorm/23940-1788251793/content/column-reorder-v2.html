Column reorder — drag a column header

Real drag, not swatches. Grab any column by its header — that is the handle the shipping component already exposes — and move it. Switch the lift and drag again; the readout reports what the value actually did to the column you moved. The neighbours now slide as they make room rather than teleporting, at 160ms — deliberately shorter than the 180ms settle, so the board is ready for the next drag before the animation is admired.
