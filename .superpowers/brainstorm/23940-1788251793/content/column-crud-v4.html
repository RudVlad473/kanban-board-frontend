A softer secondary hover, and a rail that stops running away

1 · How grey should the hover be?

The border moves in all three — it is the fill that differs. Channel deltas against white: 2.0% → 5/5/5, 2.5% → 6/6/6, 4.0% → 10/10/9 (v3's, too hard). The shipped original was 5/4/1, uneven and blue-tinted, which is part of why it read as nothing at all.

2.0% — 5/5/5 2.5% — 6/6/6 (recommended) 4.0% — 10/10/9, v3's

2 · Reaching the rail on a scrolling board

Scroll each board to its right end and try to hover the rail. A is v3: the rail grows on hover, which lengthens the track, which moves the scroll end further away — you chase a target that retreats as you approach it.
