The constraints that make it defensible here and nowhere else.
• Pointer-driven, never scroll-driven. Scroll parallax decouples apparent position from real position, which is intolerable on a surface where you scroll to find something. Nothing on this page scrolls.
• The left column is completely fixed. Headline, buttons and the sign-in link never move — you are never asked to click a moving target.
• 7px maximum across four depth layers (grid 0.25× through the cropped Backlog column at 1.15×). Past about 12px it stops reading as depth and starts reading as drift; the slider goes to 20 so you can see where that line is.
• Disabled outright under reduced motion. Not shortened — parallax is the canonical vestibular trigger, and it carries no information, so there is nothing lost by removing it.
• It never touches a work surface. No parallax on the board, sidebar, modals or forms. This is the one page whose job is to impress rather than to be operated.
