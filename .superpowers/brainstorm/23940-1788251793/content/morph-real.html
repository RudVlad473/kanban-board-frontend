Why it cannot reflow, in one line: the card's box is never mutated. The browser animates a snapshot of the card's rect toward a snapshot of the modal's rect, in the view-transition overlay layer above the page. The list underneath is untouched, which is exactly why this is worth reaching for instead of hand-rolling a FLIP morph — a hand-rolled one really would have to pull the card out of flow and leave a placeholder.
Two real edge cases this raises, both worth writing into the spec.
• Name uniqueness. view-transition-name must be unique per document state, so it is applied to the clicked card only, never to every card. Naming all 12 (or 200) would both break the transition and cost real snapshot time.
• Closing after a scroll. If you scroll the column while the modal is open, the card's rect may be off-screen on close and the morph would animate toward nothing. The guard is to check the card's rect against the viewport at close time and skip the morph if it is not visible — falling back to the plain fade. Try it: open a card, scroll the list, then close.
