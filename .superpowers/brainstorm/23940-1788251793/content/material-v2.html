Left — why it feels wrong
  • transform: translateY(-1px) on hover
  • Gap above the card grows, gap below shrinks — the row appears to breathe
  • Nothing has actually resized; it is pure perception, which is worse, because you cannot point at it
  • Borrowed from the soft-depth language, where cards are objects that physically rise
Right — the fix
  • No transform, no shadow change — only colour changes
  • Border goes #E4EBFA → #C3CEE6, surface goes #FFFFFF → #FAFBFE
  • The card is still obviously interactive, and the column is dead still
  • Free bonus: colour-only transitions never trigger layout or paint invalidation on neighbours
This is worth promoting to a rule for the whole phase. In a precision language, hover and focus states change colour only — never position, never size, never shadow. Geometry is reserved for states where something genuinely moved: drag, reorder, insert, delete. That reservation is what will make the drag choreography land, because motion will mean something by then.
