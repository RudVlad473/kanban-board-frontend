Column create, rename, delete, reorder — G9

The column is never in two places at once: the slot repopulates when the panel arrives, not after a fixed delay, so a long drop no longer shows the column twice mid-flight. The dashed slot paints under every column it crosses. The carried panel keeps its kebab at pickup and sheds it on the way down, so the column's own kebab takes over rather than appearing beside it. The dashed slot is lane-tall from the first frame and fades in over 140ms. The ghost column is replaced by a 34px rail plus + Column in the header, reachable at any scroll position.

Create, rename, delete — one surface

The rail and + Column both create the column immediately and drops its header straight into edit mode — no modal, matching the board rename decision. Enter commits, Esc removes an unnamed column. Rename does the same to an existing header. Delete confirms first, then the board closes up.
