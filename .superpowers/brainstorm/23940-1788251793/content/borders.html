The real fix is structural, not a nicer hex. State borders should be defined as a fixed step away from --border in whichever theme is active — not as color.purple.500 and color.red.500, which are fill colours chosen to carry white text at 4.5:1. A 1px hairline on a resting card has no contrast obligation at all: it is decoration, and the state it signals is always carried by something else too (the toast, the aria-busy, the card's position). That is why it can afford to be this quiet, and it is why level 1 looked wrong in both themes at once.
