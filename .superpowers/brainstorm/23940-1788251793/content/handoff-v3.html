Whatever you land on applies to the board→board transition too. They are the same mechanism — a Suspense boundary per column plus a CSS enter animation — so letting them differ would mean the same event feels like two different things depending on whether you arrived by navigation or by first load.

Why "no stagger" is still on the table. The stagger's justification was honesty about a real per-column wait. If the columns resolve together in practice (one RSC payload, one flush), a stagger is decoration pretending to be latency — and the argument that killed the strength meter applies here too. Worth deciding on what the data actually does rather than on how it looks at 3 columns.
