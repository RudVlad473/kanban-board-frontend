Keeps the UI-SPEC's "single focal point" instruction but actually composes around it: a brand lockup above, a masked dot grid so the background is a surface rather than a void, a real sub-heading, and the card at proper density. Much cheaper than A, changes no route structure, and is hard to get wrong.
What both fix, regardless of which you pick. A brand mark and product name exist (today: zero svg/img on the page). The primary action is a button rather than a text link. Fields have placeholders. There is a sub-heading giving the screen a voice. And the theme toggle stops floating 120px below the card as an orphan — in A it sits in the left column's footer, in B it pins to the frame corner.
Deliberately not offered: the blurred-product-backdrop treatment. Signed-in board UI behind glass is the obvious "modern auth screen" move, and it is exactly the atmospheric vocabulary ruled out on screen 1. Offering it as a co-equal option would quietly reopen a settled decision.
