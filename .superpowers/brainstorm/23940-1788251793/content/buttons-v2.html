Pending — greyed, four ways

All four keep the crawl rail and the spinner, sit 2px down with the bottom edge gone, and never collapse to a ghost. They differ only in where the grey comes from.

A — neutral fill, dark text. Grey surface, muted label, accent kept only in the rail and spinner. The most obviously inert.

B — grey fill, white text. Same weight and contrast as the live button, all accent drained. Reads as the same control, temporarily neutral.

C — desaturated purple. Keeps a trace of the accent so it still reads as that button, not a different one.

D — filter: grayscale. One line, applies to every variant including destructive with no extra tokens. Cheapest to ship, least controllable.
