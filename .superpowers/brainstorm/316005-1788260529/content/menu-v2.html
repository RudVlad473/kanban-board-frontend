Which of the two readings of “instant” do you mean?

3 — front-loaded fade (my recommendation)

Legible 55ms after the click — under 4 frames — so there is effectively no wait, but the arrival is still visible rather than a blink. Exit gets the longer 120ms, where slowness costs the user nothing because they have already moved on.

4 — instant in, animated out

The literal reading: opening costs zero time, and all the motion lives on the close where it is free. Also the most consistent with Base UI's own data-instant philosophy. Cost: opening a menu — the more common action — is the one with no craft in it.
