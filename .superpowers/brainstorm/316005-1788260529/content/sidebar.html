A — full hide (recommended)

What the mock specifies and what is built today. Keeps the accent-reservation entry exactly as UI-SPEC records it. Reclaims all 300px. Cost: the expand control is an orphan pill in a corner nothing else occupies.

B — 72px icon rail

Nothing vanishes; the expand control stays where the panel was; active board stays visible while collapsed. Cost: this is new layout language, which the spec puts out of scope — and it reclaims 228px instead of 300px.

Two things I measured that change the build

1 — cost of animating the real layout (running app, 6 columns / 72 cards) collapse 300→283→217→150→83→17→1 median 16.7ms p95 16.9 max 17.1 frames>20ms: 0 expand 1→17→83→150→217→283→300 median 16.7ms p95 16.9 max 17.1 frames>20ms: 0 → animating the honest layout is free. No FLIP machinery, no transform faking. 2 — the panel's own text reflows on the way out "ALL BOARDS (3)" caption height 66px → 102px (wraps to 2 lines at navW ≈ 133) → so the panel gets a fixed-width inner inside overflow:hidden, and its contents slide out of the clip intact instead of squashing and re-wrapping mid-flight. That is also what keeps rule 4 (text never crossfades) satisfied: the strings move behind a mask rather than changing shape.
